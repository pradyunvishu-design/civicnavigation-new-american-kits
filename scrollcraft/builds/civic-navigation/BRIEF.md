# Civic Navigation landing journey

## Product and audience

Civic Navigation is a student-led service that helps immigrant and refugee families find verified local support in Houston and across Texas. The landing page must establish trust quickly, make the service understandable without jargon, and end with a direct path to ZIP-based help.

## Creative interview

- **Vibe:** premium-minimal, calm, civic, trustworthy, human.
- **Reference material:** the primary supplied luminous watercolor relief map and its existing generated map film.
- **Journey:** orientation → recognize a need → understand the verification layer → see the statewide network → take action.
- **Energy curve:** calm opening → recognition → mild tension → quiet peak and relief → confident close.
- **Memorable moment:** the routes converge on Houston and the visual network resolves into a usable next step.
- **Unique mechanic:** the same uninterrupted map gradually tilts from a flat editorial field into a horizontal, dimensional civic atlas as the visitor scrolls.
- **Aesthetic direction:** editorial civic cartography on warm paper; no dark tech gradient, floating dashboard cards, generic chapter counters, or decorative 3D objects.
- **World grammar:** one continuous worldflight with a single fixed atlas surface. There are no image swaps, cross-dissolved legs, or stacked landing-page sections.
- **Owned assets:** the primary supplied map artwork, its generated map video, and the Civic Navigation logo.

## Belief and action

- **What the page should make someone believe:** verified local help can be findable, understandable, and close enough to act on.
- **Primary action:** Find help by Texas ZIP.
- **Secondary action:** Read the step-by-step guides.
- **Tell-someone sentence:** “It’s the site where the Texas help network draws itself under your hand and lands at your ZIP.”

## Feeling curve

| Beat | Feeling | Visual and copy role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | Calm | National map, large breathing room, short promise. |
| Recognition | Seen | The same map gains depth while plain-language family needs appear. |
| Friction | Tension | The atlas lowers toward a more horizontal plane; verified details matter. |
| Connection | Relief | Routes brighten across the same surface as the network becomes tangible. |
| Landing | Confidence | The single map settles into a dimensional civic platform behind two practical actions and an email contact. |

The authored silence is the brief transition before the routes converge. It should feel deliberate, not empty.

## Responsive contract

- Desktop and laptop keep copy in the left negative space and the route focal point to the right.
- Phone uses a compact copy band, a mobile crop of the same map, and 44px minimum tap targets.
- Reduced-motion users receive the complete story over the same static poster; scrub video and the 3D tilt are disabled.
- Long translations may wrap freely without being clipped or forced into a fixed-height panel.

## Performance contract

- Scroll motion is driven by Scroll Craft’s single smoothed playhead.
- Scrub assets use H.264, 30 FPS, dense GOP/keyframes, no audio, and a mobile-specific encode.
- The flight has one segment, one poster, and one map clip. CSS perspective turns that single visual into a more horizontal plane without loading another image.
